Tick Path Highlighting
Active nodes glow: ●yellow=running, ●green=success, ●red=failure. Inactive nodes dim.

Utility Nodes
U? Utility Selector picks child with highest utility score. Set scores with Set Utility action nodes.
U→ Utility Sequence executes children in order of score.

Resize any pane
Every border between panes is a drag handle. Drag left/right on the vertical bars between palette, canvas, and properties. Drag the horizontal bar between the canvas and sim up/down. Drag the bar between the sim world and the log panel left/right.

Drag robots
Click and drag any robot circle in the sim panel to reposition it. If you drop it near a waypoint it will snap and update its target.

Waypoint editing
In View mode, right-click a waypoint in the sim to rename or delete it. In Waypoint mode, click to place.

FOV / Sensor / Comm
Sliders in the sim toolbar control field-of-view angle, sensor detection range, and communication range. See the Sensor tab for per-robot FOV.
